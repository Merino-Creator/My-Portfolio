WHO´S DANIEL?

About me

Greetings, I´m Daniel.
After beeing interested in coding for a long time I was finally able to start off my career as a Frontend Developer.
My passion for coding comes from beeing able to create or realize ideas and thoughts into something accessable.
A lot of my inspiration comes from a gaming point of view. Often I found myself fascinated by the possibilities of world building for example.
And just like building a world in a game, a website is also a place you build and make it as enjoyable as possible for the user.
So by every world I build, I learn how to make it better and more exciting for the future.

Let´s collaborate and build the world you are looking for together!
